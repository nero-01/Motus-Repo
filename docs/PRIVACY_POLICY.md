## 1. Introduction

MotusTots ("we", "our", or "the app") is a family management application that helps parents and caregivers organize chores, rewards, education, routines, and co-parenting. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

By using MotusTots, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** Email address and password (or other sign-in method) when you create an account.
- **Profile information:** Name and optional profile details you enter for yourself and family members.
- **Family and child data:** Names, ages, and other details you add for children and family members to use features such as education, chores, rewards, and routines.
- **Content you create:** Chores, routines, meal plans, calendar events, messages, and similar data you enter in the app.
- **Photos:** If you use the image picker (e.g. for reminders or avatars), we process images you select from your device. We do not access your full photo library without your explicit action.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage and device information:** Such as device type, operating system, and app version, to support and improve the app.
- **Logs and errors:** Technical information (e.g. crash reports) to fix issues. We do not log sensitive personal data in production.

### 2.3 Data we do not collect

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not use your data for advertising or third-party marketing.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Provide and personalize app features (chores, rewards, education, routines, co-parenting, meal planning, analytics).
- Store and sync your family and child data so you can use the app across devices.
- Improve the app, fix bugs, and ensure security.
- Comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms.

---

## 4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

- Your data is stored using **Supabase** (database and authentication), which provides secure, encrypted storage and is hosted in accordance with Supabase’s own security and privacy practices.
- We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.

---

## 5. Sharing of Information

- We do **not** sell or rent your personal information.
- We may share data only:
  - **Within your family:** Features such as co-parenting and shared calendars are designed to share information between members you invite.
  - **With service providers:** We use Supabase and related infrastructure to run the app. These providers are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  - **When required by law:** If required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- We retain your account and app data for as long as your account is active.
- If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data in line with our data retention practices and applicable law.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access and correction:** You can view and update your profile and family data in the app.
- **Deletion:** You may request account deletion (e.g. via in-app settings or by contacting us). We will process such requests in line with applicable law.
- **Opt-out:** You can stop using the app and delete your account at any time.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or other regions with specific privacy laws, you may have additional rights (e.g. access, portability, restriction, objection). Contact us to exercise them.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

MotusTots is intended for use by parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parental consent. The child-related data in the app is provided by you, the account holder, and is used only to provide the services you request.
