### Full description (<= 4000 chars)
MotusTots helps families stay organized, build routines, and support early learning in one place.

Use MotusTots to:
- Plan and manage family routines.
- Track reminders and weekly planner tasks.
- Support learning with age-appropriate activities and worksheets.
- Coordinate with co-parents through shared family workflows.
- Keep household information and progress accessible in one app.

Built for busy families, MotusTots brings structure to day-to-day life while keeping the experience simple and child-friendly.

Core features:
- Family setup and child profiles
- Routine and reminders management
- Learning activities and worksheets
- Co-parenting support tools
- Progress and planning utilities

Privacy and support:
- Privacy policy: https://motustots.com/privacy
- Support: support@motustots.com

Feature availability may vary by release stage and account setup.
